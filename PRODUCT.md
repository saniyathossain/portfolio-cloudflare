# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences, all confirmed by the owner: hiring managers and recruiters who skim for seniority and decide whether to reach out; potential clients and collaborators who judge skills and past work and decide whether to start a project; engineering peers who check the stack, the articles and general credibility.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Mohammad Saniyat Hossain (Staff Software Engineer) at saniyat.com. It presents his CV content (profile, skills, experience, education, selected articles) and turns interest into contact. Success is a visitor opening the contact modal or emailing.

## Positioning

A single owner-authored profile whose content mirrors one source of truth (`public/assets/data/portfolio.json`, derived from `docs/cv-modern-template.md`), delivered as a fast static page that is also itself evidence of engineering craft.

## Operating Context

Static Astro HTML with Preact islands, served by a Cloudflare Worker; deployed with `./deploy.sh` (Wrangler). Contact requests go to `POST /api/contact` (rate-limited, honeypot, optional Turnstile). Article list is fetched at build time from dev.to and Medium; there are no runtime third-party requests by default.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single content source in `portfolio.json`; `<head>` is generated by `scripts/sync-head.ts`.
- Light-only theme; every animation gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`; responsive verified at 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1440.
- Keep Google PageSpeed, SEO and CSP intact; no new network requests (fonts and icons are local).
- All failures (404, 500, 502, 503, offline) render the site-themed outage page from `src/content/errors.json`.
- Soft per-section "under construction" notices via `site.features.underConstruction.<key>`; site-wide maintenance mode via the Worker `MAINTENANCE_MODE` variable.
- Analytics is opt-in; empty means zero requests.

## Brand Commitments

Owner name and short name from `portfolio.json`. No years-of-experience or headline figures in copy (owner rule). Existing site look is owned by the owner and recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`; this file does not define it.

## Evidence on Hand

Real CV content in `portfolio.json` and `docs/cv-modern-template.md`; owner photo and company/school logos in `public/assets/img`; articles pulled from the owner's dev.to and Medium accounts (new posts start inactive until the owner curates them). Never fabricate CV content, testimonials, clients or metrics.

## Product Principles

1. Contact and credibility first: every section serves a recruiter, client or peer deciding in seconds.
2. Content is truthful and single-sourced; nothing is invented to fill space.
3. Performance and privacy are part of the product: Lighthouse 100, no third-party requests by default.
4. Accessibility and reduced motion are never traded for effect.
5. Behaviour of existing flows stays exact unless the owner asks for a change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere; keep keyboard and screen-reader paths for the nav overlay and contact dialog. No other formal standard has been stated by the owner.
